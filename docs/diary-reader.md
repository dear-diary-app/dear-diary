# Wider diary reading

Published own/partner Read routes use a compact owner/date header with a back link, song at the top, small sheet indicator/arrows, full available mobile width and the existing bottom navigation. History keeps its calendar and entry list, with Open full-screen reader for each readable entry; that modal includes its own bottom navigation and supports Close/Escape/Back. Long content remains vertically scrollable.

Horizontal touch swipes across a reading sheet turn its logical pages; vertical intent, multiple fingers, text selection and edge gestures do not trigger a page turn. Existing sheet boundaries, bundled typography proportions and sticker coordinates are preserved. Larger available width enlarges both text and stickers together, without re-pagination that could move stickers to different text. Writing/editor layout and sticker editing gestures are unchanged. Shared app-tab swipes already exclude journal regions; reader marks its surface explicitly as excluded too.

Synthetic real touch tests at 320/390/820 in paper/midnight passed left/right page turning, vertical movement without page turning, fallback arrows, no horizontal overflow and history reader Escape/Back/bottom navigation. Visual previews inspected. No real content or accounts used, no physical-device testing. The screenshot supplied for reference was deleted.
