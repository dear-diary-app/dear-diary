# GK starter bank

34 English questions, reviewed 2026-11-27. No live AI or paid question service.

- Space, 18 questions: NASA, https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/planets/ . Planet order and relative sizes, planet count, 2006 Pluto reclassification, Ceres, giant planet groups, exoplanets. Correct choices were checked against the matching statements on the fetched NASA page.
- Science, 14 questions: BIPM, https://www.bipm.org/en/measurement-units/si-base-units . Seven base quantities, unit names and symbols, checked against its table. These are related questions, not 14 unrelated facts.
- World, 2 questions: United Nations, https://www.un.org/en/our-work/official-languages . Six languages and Arabic membership, checked against its explicit list.

Each JSON row carries a source and checked date. Distractors are other choices, four distinct options with one correct index. Migration seeds are generated from this JSON; the browser never imports it. No claim of 200+ questions or broad GK coverage. No current-affairs questions. Source checking lowers error risk, not a guarantee. Source link is shown after reveal. Public source code and repeat play mean this is a casual quiz, not an anti-cheat exam.

The server skips the ten most recent questions when other questions exist. After that, repeats are possible. No scoring, leaderboard or money. Invite appears while both are in Chat, not as push. Presence is the app's best-effort 35-second lease. Invitation expires after 60 seconds, ready check after 30 seconds; both need recent readiness (8 seconds) and foreground Chat. Three-second shared pre-start, ten server seconds. One first answer per player, both revealed only after deadline. A disconnected player can miss the question. No promise of frame-perfect simultaneous display. Results are recoverable for five minutes in the UI; rows persist in the database until a future retention policy is agreed.
